# AI Code of Conduct

I use AI to build this website, but I try not to use it for the writing itself. There is only one exception: the [GCC internals](/articles/Inside-Internals/gcc-internals) article. For that one I recorded my thoughts, sent the transcript to an AI, and had it turn that into the article and I also used AI to make the ASCII art, instead of pasting an image and complicating things. For every other article I've written, and for the ones I'm going to write, AI is only involved in generating ASCII art examples. It never changes my explanation.

You might find spelling and grammar mistakes here, since I'm not a native English speaker.

Finally, my workflow: I write in Affine, convert to Markdown files, and statically compile those to HTML. I use Affine for writing, ASCII art for simple concepts (it's easier to understand), and Excalidraw(Sometimes Affine) for drawings. As I already mentioned, ASCII art is the only thing that is AI-generated.

Thanks for your attention.
